Games Played affects the outcome only if a Play Gem accessory is equipped.

Configure the simulation to allow purchasing HP Recovery in battle. Provide either the Max Recoveries you allow, or tick Max from Money and provide the character's Money on hand when entering the fight to compute the max automatically, where cost = 0.5×money + 0.5×maxHP + 3000.

When recoveries are available, the simulation finds a recommended threshold to purchase one, and buys it as soon as HP falls below that threshold. Like in the actual game, the recovery is applied after one additional attack from the enemy. Select Override threshold to use your own threshold rather than solving for it.

Enter the base stats you have already acquired from levelling. Do not include extra stats from equipment or bonuses.

Extra Stat Points are allocated on top of the base stats using the allocation plan defined under Extra Stat Allocations, where each Half takes half of what is left and All takes the rest, the same as the real game. With Auto Compute on in Simulation settings, the chance to win follows the slider as you drag it. The extra stats a specific win rate needs can be found for you by the Solver.

Set the gear your character is wearing. The ten Slot numbers each hold a different set, to allow for quickly comparing different loadouts.

Drag accessories between slots to swap them, and hold Alt while dragging to copy an accessory from one slot to another.

Max error margin sets how precise the answer is, and sampling stops as soon as it is reached. A tighter (smaller) margin will increase computational load. Tick Fixed trials to give a number of battles to run instead, and take whatever margin that many happens to buy.

The Auto Compute option lets the answer automatically update whenever changes are made (stats, equipment, etc.), but is force disabled under a ±0.1% margin, or over 500,000 trials, to avoid too much CPU usage. A long run can be called off with the Stop button it turns into, which also turns Auto Compute off.

Click Solve to find the approximate minimum number of extra stats to allocate to get at least the Target win %. May be computationally heavy especially with a low Max error margin and if HP Recovery is available.
